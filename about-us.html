At Dèpendance, we believe that meaningful solutions arise from the interplay of simplicity and exploration, where curiosity and collaboration guide ideas to unfold naturally.  

Whether crafting digital interfaces or immersive audiovisual performances, we approach each project with an open mind and a balance of thoughtful design and technical expertise.
As a small, young-run studio, collaboration is at the heart of our work. Partnering with like-minded realities inspires us to share creativity, foster growth, and create impactful results.

By blending experimentation with refinement, we strive to craft experiences that resonate, always keeping the human experience at the center.
