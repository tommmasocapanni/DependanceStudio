General Description*
Computer Generated
Memories
Computer Generated Memories is an audiovisual installation created for the 2024 edition of Charta Festival, exhibited at Fondazione Pastificio Cerere in Rome. Although initially developed for the festival only, the project was later showcased in Anagni, Italy, at the newly restored "Palazzo della Cultura”.

The project explores humanity’s disconnection from the natural world, focusing on how daily practices and constructed environments foster an illusion of superiority and control over nature. Through a visually evocative narrative, it invites reflection on the fragility of these constructs.

Technical Description*
Computer Generated
Memories
Developed in TouchDesigner, the installation merges point clouds with curated sound design to create an immersive ambient experience. By visually deconstructing human-altered spaces, the project highlights their inherent vulnerability, presenting a fragmented view of environments shaped by anthropocentric practices.

The result is a contemplative piece that bridges technology and ecology through art.
