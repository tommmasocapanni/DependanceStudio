General Description*
Regal // Involve Records
Our ongoing collaboration with Berlin-based Involve Records has been an exciting exploration of music and visual design. Known for its cutting-edge EDM releases, the label represents artists like Regal, a Spanish powerhouse in the club scene.

Together, we’ve worked on dynamic visuals that complement their bold musical identity and help bring their vision to life in the digital space.

Project-Specific*
Regal // Involve Records
For Involve Records, we combined 3D rendering, animation, and 2D Photoshop design to craft vinyl center covers and animated Instagram reels. These visuals aim to reflect the energy of Regal’s music, translating sound into compelling visuals that resonate with the label’s aesthetic.

The result is a collaborative effort to bring the club experience into the digital world with sharp designs and thoughtful animations.
