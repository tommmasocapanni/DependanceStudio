General Description*
Spooky Facotry Inc.
Spooky Factory Inc. is a young streetwear brand with a mission to foster a vibrant, inclusive community through its unisex clothing line.

Known for its bold hand-drawn illustrations and strong digital presence, the brand blends artistic expression with contemporary fashion.

Spooky Factory isn’t just about clothing—it’s about creating a shared identity and inviting everyone to be part of its growing, creative community.

Website-Specific*
Spooky Facotry Inc.
The website design reflects Spooky Factory’s unique blend of fashion and artistry, combining sleek, minimalist layouts reminiscent of high-end brands with playful storytelling elements.

Hand-drawn stickers, dynamic illustrations, and animated hovers were thoughtfully integrated to create a more engaging and interactive browsing experience.

These elements immerse users in the Spooky Identity, transforming the site into more than just an online store—it’s an invitation to explore and become part of the Spooky world.
