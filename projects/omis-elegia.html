General Description*
From The Archaic To
The Fast Self
Omis Elegia is a sound designer and musician who crafts timeless synthwave experiences by blending retro and contemporary sounds. Our ongoing collaboration focuses on shaping a visual identity that contrasts his abstract musical style.

Instead of echoing his synthwave roots, the visuals highlight a distinctly millennial aesthetic, celebrating influences from video games, surrealism, and personal storytelling.

Project-Specific*
From The Archaic To
The Fast Self
The visual identity combines immersive 3D landscapes with surreal elements, drawing from the artist’s life and influences. Heavy use of video game-inspired motifs merges with abstract designs to create a rich, multi-dimensional visual language.

The aim is to craft a narrative that complements Omis Elegia’s music while presenting an unexpected and thought-provoking contrast to his soundscapes.
