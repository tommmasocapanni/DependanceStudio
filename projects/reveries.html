General Description*
Reveries
Reveries is a record label, an events organization and a broadcasting platform active in the world of clubbing, based in Rome. We collaborate with professionals and artists from different fields to make our contents primarily oriented towards the musical aspect and the artistic expressiveness.  Our intent is to investigate contemporary soundscapes by promoting the growth of emergent realities and generating parties where space contains time and time flows through dance.

Technical Description*
Reveries
For each performance, we developed visuals tailored to Reveries' dynamic soundscapes, combining a variety of techniques and styles.
The visuals included AI-generated videos, 3D renderings, and internet-inspired aesthetics, all designed to enhance the mood and atmosphere of the music.

These were projected live using Resolume Arena and mapped with precision onto a 1920x1080 display, ensuring a seamless integration of sound and imagery throughout each event.
