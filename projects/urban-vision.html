Plasticity is the visual identity project developed for Urban Heart, the event format by Urban Vision. based in Rome. We collaborate with professionals and artists from different fields to make our contents primarily oriented towards the musical aspect and the artistic expressiveness.  Our intent is to investigate contemporary soundscapes by promoting the growth of emergent realities and generating parties where space contains time and time flows through dance.
