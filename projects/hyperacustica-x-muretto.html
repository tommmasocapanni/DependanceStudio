General Description*
Hyperacustica x D
Over several months, we collaborated with Hyperacustica, an Italian electronic music collective, to curate visuals for their live performances in San Lorenzo, Rome’s art district. These performances aimed to create immersive and engaging experiences for audiences, blending sound and visuals in innovative ways.

It was a rewarding opportunity to contribute to a collective deeply rooted in experimental music and visual art.

Technical Description*
Hyperacustica x D
For each performance, we developed visuals tailored to Hyperacustica’s dynamic soundscapes, combining a variety of techniques and styles.
The visuals included AI-generated videos, 3D renderings, and internet-inspired aesthetics, all designed to enhance the mood and atmosphere of the music.

These were projected live using Resolume Arena and mapped with precision onto a 1920x1080 display, ensuring a seamless integration of sound and imagery throughout each event.
