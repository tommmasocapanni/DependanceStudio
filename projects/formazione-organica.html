Concept*
Formazione : Organica
formazione:organica is a visual exploration of natural forms immersed in a surreal, dreamlike setting.
‍
The project simulates an idealized environment where undisturbed evolution unfolds without external interference, evoking a sense of timeless suspension. Presented as part of a live audiovisual performance at LPM Rome, the work combines abstract storytelling with a meditative atmosphere.

Technical Description*
Formazione : Organica
This series features 3D-rendered photogrammetries and meticulously modeled landscapes, designed to create a cohesive and immersive audiovisual experience. The simulated environment emphasizes organic textures and surreal aesthetics, blending digital artistry with natural inspiration.

Developed for live projection, formazione:organica offers a unique interplay of visual and auditory elements tailored for performance settings.
